# Explore Pakistan 🇵🇰 - Modern Travel & Tourism Portal

A modern, responsive, and beautiful travel platform showcasing Pakistan's breathtaking cities, alpine mountain peaks, luxury heritage hotels, cultural heritage, and culinary treasures.

![Explore Pakistan Banner](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1589182373726-e4f658ab50f0?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

### 1. 🏙️ Explore Pakistani Cities
Comprehensive, verified city cards with popular attractions, visiting hours, entry fees, and iconic food specialties:
- **Karachi** (City of Lights): Clifton Beach, Mazar-e-Quaid, Port Grand, Sea View
- **Lahore** (Cultural Heart): Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila), Minar-e-Pakistan, Shalimar Gardens
- **Islamabad** (Green Capital): Faisal Mosque, Daman-e-Koh, Pakistan Monument, Rawal Lake
- **Peshawar** (Historic Gateway): Qissa Khwani Bazaar, Mahabat Khan Mosque, Bala Hissar Fort, Sethi House
- **Skardu** (Gateway to K2): Shangrila Resort, Upper Kachura Lake, Sarfaranga Cold Desert, Deosai Plains
- **Hunza Valley**: Baltit Fort, Altit Fort, Attabad Lake, Passu Cones, Hussaini Bridge
- **Multan** (City of Saints): Tomb of Shah Rukn-e-Alam, Tomb of Bahauddin Zakariya, Multan Fort, Blue Pottery
- **Quetta** (Fruit Garden): Hanna Lake, Hazarganji Chiltan National Park, Ziarat Residency, Kandhari Bazaar
- **Swat Valley** (Switzerland of the East): Malam Jabba Ski Resort & Zipline, White Palace, Mahodand Lake
- **Faisalabad** (Textile Capital): Clock Tower & Eight Bazaars, Gumti Fountain, Gatwala Park, D-Ground
- **Murree & Galyat** (Queen of Hills): Mall Road Murree, Patriata Cable Car, Kashmir Point, Pipeline Track

### 2. 🔍 Real-Time Live Destination Search
- Instant autocomplete dropdown searching across all cities, regions, and attractions.
- Experience type and region filters.

### 3. 💰 Interactive Travel Budget Calculator
- Dynamic estimation of total trip cost in **PKR (₨)** and **USD ($)**.
- Sliders for duration (3–21 days) and group size (1–6 travelers).
- Travel tiers: Backpacker, Standard Comfort, and 5-Star Luxury.
- Real-time cost breakdown for Accommodation, Food, Transportation, and Excursions.

### 4. 🏨 Luxury Hotels & Resorts Reservation
- Verified luxury stays across Pakistan (Islamabad Serena, PC Lahore, Beach Luxury Karachi, Luxus Hunza, Shangrila Skardu, PC Malam Jabba, PC Bhurban, PC Peshawar, Quetta Serena).
- Interactive booking modal with date pickers, room categories, and instant simulation.

### 5. 💱 Dynamic Currency Switcher
- Instant conversion across the entire website between **PKR (₨)** and **USD ($)**.

### 6. ❤️ Saved Places Wishlist
- Save favorite destinations and cities with persistent `localStorage` storage and slide-out drawer.

### 7. 📸 High-Definition Gallery & Lightbox
- Category-filtered photography (Architecture, Mountains, Lakes, Coastline).
- Interactive lightbox with previous/next controls and keyboard navigation.

### 8. 📘 Comprehensive Travel Guide
- Pakistan E-Visa requirements (Nadra portal info).
- Best seasons and weather advice.
- Culture, etiquette, and packing checklist.
- Emergency contact numbers (Tourist Police 1422, Rescue 1122).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5**: Semantic and accessible markup.
- **CSS3**: Custom design system inspired by Pakistan's green and white flag (`#01411C`, `#0A5C28`, `#10B981`, white, gold brass accents).
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Modular application architecture (`data.js` and `app.js`) with zero external dependencies.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ittrackinstitute0-lgtm/travel-website.git
   cd travel-website
   ```

2. Open in browser:
   - Simply double click `index.html`, or
   - Run a local server:
     ```bash
     python -m http.server 8000
     ```
   - Open `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.
