📘 Essential Tips

Pakistan Travel Guide

Everything international and domestic travelers need to know for a seamless, enjoyable, and safe journey across Pakistan.

🛂

Pakistan E-Visa Guide

Citizens of 191 countries can obtain an official tourist e-visa online via the Nadra portal (visa.nadra.gov.pk). Processing takes 48 to 72 hours with basic passport scans and hotel reservation slips. Over 120 nations qualify for Visa on Arrival.

💵

Currency & Payments

The Pakistani Rupee (PKR) is the official currency. Major credit cards (Visa & MasterCard) work seamlessly in hotels, malls, and restaurants in Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi. Carry cash for northern valley bazaars, fruit stalls, and tips.

🤝

Culture & Etiquette

Pakistani people are renowned for hospitality, often treating tourists as honored guests. Modest dress covering shoulders and knees is customary. A scarf or shawl is helpful for women when entering sacred mosques like Badshahi or Faisal Mosque.

Cultural & Skiing

Delightful cool weather for exploring Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad; skiing at Malam Jabba, Swat.
